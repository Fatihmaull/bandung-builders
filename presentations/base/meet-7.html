The structure

The 7-slide hackathon deck

SLIDE 1 · ~30 s

Hook

One sentence. The Bandung-specific problem and who feels it. Open with a number, image, or visceral story — not a logo.

SLIDE 2 · ~45 s

Problem

The workaround your user resorts to today. The Web2 status quo and why it fails on trust. Use real names, real places.

SLIDE 3 · ~45 s

Solution

What you built. Why it's only possible because of an on-chain primitive on Base. Be specific about the trust assumption you removed.

SLIDE 4 · ~90 s (LIVE)

Demo

Switch to the browser. Connect wallet, approve, stake, see rewards accrue. No backup video — bullet-proof your live demo or rehearse a flawless screen recording.

SLIDE 5 · ~45 s

Tech & Tokenomics

One-screen architecture diagram + one-screen tokenomics summary (from Meet 5 canvas). Don't read it. Let the audience scan.

SLIDE 6 · ~45 s

GTM & Why Base

First 100 users, then 1,000. Why Base specifically (the Coinbase moat, the fee economics). What about Bandung gives you unfair distribution?

SLIDE 7 · ~30 s

The Ask

Be precise. Are you looking for mentors? Pre-seed angels? Ecosystem grant? Co-founders? Specific asks get specific answers.

Live demo · 90 s

The 90-second demo script

Pre-stake nothing. The "from zero" walk demonstrates that the protocol works end-to-end. Type your script in the box and rehearse against the timer.

  1. 00:00–00:10
    "Here's the live dApp on Base Sepolia." Already on the landing page, wallet disconnected.
  2. 00:10–00:25
    Connect wallet via RainbowKit. Show the modal. "All major wallets, one button."
  3. 00:25–00:40
    Approve. Mention it's free on Base, ~cents. Pre-funded faucet wallet recommended.
  4. 00:40–01:00
    Stake 100 tokens. Confirm. Watch the "earned" counter start ticking up in real time.
  5. 01:00–01:15
    "Notice the counter increments." That's rewardPerToken doing its thing on Base. Block time ~2 s.
  6. 01:15–01:30
    Claim. Tx submits, confirms. Show the new wallet balance increment.
00:00
Have a backup recording

Workshop policy: in addition to live, you record a flawless 90-second demo screencast and have it ready in OBS / a browser tab. If the projector flickers or wifi drops, you say "In the interest of time, here's the recording" and never break stride.

Block · 30 min

Go-To-Market — specifically for Base

Base is not just an L2 — it is a distribution platform. A GTM that ignores this is leaving 100× of growth on the table.

1. Coinbase Wallet integration

Smart-wallet creation in-app means zero seed-phrase onboarding. Use the Onchain Kit. ~110M Coinbase users == one tap.

2. Base Builds & ecosystem grants

Quarterly programs. Apply. Even a $5–25k grant funds your first 6 months of treasury runway.

3. Farcaster + Frames

Native Base distribution channel. Embed your dApp as a Frame in casts. ~500k DAU concentrated in Web3-native users — your beachhead.

4. Cents-level gas = consumer pricing

Subsidize first 10 user txs with a paymaster. $0.10 customer-acquisition cost for someone who took an on-chain action is unbeatable.

The Bandung edge

  • Local communities are tightly-knit. Pasar / kampus / co-working group of 30 = your first cohort.
  • Mobile-first. Don't ship desktop-only — every wallet click must work on Android.
  • Rupiah price formatting. Show IDR alongside USD. Builds trust 10×.
  • Bahasa on the landing page. English in the docs is fine; the homepage greets in Bahasa.
