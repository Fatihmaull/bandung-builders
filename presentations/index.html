The 8 sessions

✓
WEEK 1 · MEET 1
Kick-Off & EVM Blockchain Mechanism
Non-Technical 2.5 hr Speaker-led

The EVM as a deterministic state machine, why L2 rollups exist, what Base is specifically, and why Bandung is the right city for this cohort. Sign the Rules of the Game.

✓
WEEK 1 · MEET 2
Fundamental EVM & Foundry Setup
Technical 2.5 hr Hands-on

Install Foundry. Decode foundry.toml. Use cast against live Base Sepolia. Tour the monorepo. Verify your AI agent is loaded with .cursorrules.

✓
WEEK 2 · MEET 3
Ideasi Proyek & Design Thinking
Non-Technical 2.5 hr Workshop

EDIPT for Web3. Pick a Bandung problem worth solving on Base. Write a problem statement with a real trust insight — not a "we'll add a token" hand-wave.

✓
WEEK 2 · MEET 4
Smart Contract Development (Solidity + Forge)
Technical 2.5 hr Live-coding

Live-code a Synthetix-style staking accumulator. Implement updateReward, stake, withdraw, getReward. Cover with vm.prank + vm.warp tests.

✓
WEEK 3 · MEET 5
DeFi Economics & Tokenomics Canvas
Non-Technical 2.5 hr Calculators

Derive earned() from first principles. Calculate APR, runway, sustainability. Fill the four-pillar Tokenomics Canvas live — Supply, Vesting, Utility, Yield.

✓
WEEK 3 · MEET 6
Frontend Connection (Viem, Wagmi, RainbowKit)
Technical 2.5 hr Live-coding

Configure providers in the correct order. Write read hooks and write hooks. Handle the full tx lifecycle in the UI. Author the missing useApproveStake hook.

✓
WEEK 4 · MEET 7
Pitching & Base Go-To-Market
Non-Technical 2.5 hr Rehearsal

The 7-slide, 5-minute pitch. The 90-second live demo. The Base-specific GTM stack. Rehearse twice in pairs. Drill the judge questions.

✓
WEEK 4 · MEET 8
Demo Day & Infrastructure
Technical 2.5 hr Ship

Deploy to Base Sepolia. Verify on Basescan. Deploy the frontend on Vercel. End-to-end smoke test. Record the demo backup. Walk through the judge's rubric.
